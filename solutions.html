Our two most complete line projects — section order written from real equipment

Pasteurization, Cooling & Air-Drying Line View configuration

Complete line structure in one view — pasteurization, cooling, elevator and drying sections.

For bottled juices and bagged pickled vegetables: continuous pasteurization, cooling and surface air-drying of packaged product.

Section order: bottled/bagged product infeed → pasteurization tank → transverse cooling tank → elevator → surface air-drying fans → discharge to downstream packing.

Configured per project: package type (bottle or pouch), temperature zones, line speed and water supply are confirmed against your package and process. Air-drying here means surface water on the package, not drying of food inside the package.

Fresh-Cut Vegetable Washing, Cutting & Air-Drying Line View configuration

Real line photographed on site — sections are configured per project.

For fresh-cut produce preparation: foreign-matter removal, cutting, washing, dewatering and surface air-drying.

Section order: inspection & foreign-matter removal → cutting → washing → dewatering → surface air-drying. Leafy greens and root vegetables follow two different configurations; whether cutting comes before or after washing depends on your produce and cut specification.

Configured per project: tell us your produce (leafy or root), cut sizes, target surface dryness and hourly volume. Washing and air-drying sections are quoted per your actual line, not presented as one fixed unit.

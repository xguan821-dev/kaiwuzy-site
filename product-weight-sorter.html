Tray-Based Weight Sorter

One piece per tray — weighed individually, discharged by grade

Tray-type grader for round fruit and similar produce. Each piece rides in its own tray, is weighed on the conveyor and released into separate grade outlets — watch the real fruit sorting demo below (played at 0.8x speed). Grading accuracy, sorting speed and throughput are confirmed against your fruit and line requirements, not assumed from this demo.

  • One piece per tray — individual weighing, no bulk weighing
  • Weight grades discharge into separate outlets (visible on the sample unit)
  • Stainless frame with adjustable feet; control cabinet mounted above the tray line
  • Configuration quoted per fruit size, grade count and throughput
